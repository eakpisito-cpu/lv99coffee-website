Our story · Since 2023

From a shipping shop
to a neighborhood cafe

LV99coffee opened in 2023 in a space that had previously been a shipping shop run by the owner's wife. Around the same time, the cafe where the owner had enjoyed coffee for years closed and moved to Chiang Rai. That moment of change became the beginning of a coffee shop he had long wanted to create.

Having lived and worked around Sam Yot and Charoen Krung for many years, the owner wanted the shop to belong naturally to the neighborhood: a welcoming stop where people can return for a good cup every day.

LV99 comes from Level 99 — in gaming, the highest level and the idea of going all the way in what you do. Here, it means giving every cup our full attention.

The details in every glass

The coffee we drink every day,
made with care in every detail

The owner and his family are regular customers too. What we serve you is made to the same standard we choose for ourselves, from the strength of the coffee to every piece of ice.

23g

A full coffee dose

We use 23 grams of coffee for each extraction, giving the drink a full, rich coffee character without reducing the dose simply to lower the price.

Pacamara

Beans we trust

We use beans from Pacamara Coffee Roasters and work to keep the flavor consistent, so regular customers can return to the cup they know.

Filtered water

Ice made in-house

Our ice is produced in the shop from filtered water. Filters are replaced regularly, so we can look after the process from the water source to the ice in your glass.
